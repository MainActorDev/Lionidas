## Variant C — coverage-strip

### Design stance
One verdict LINE (no hero), then a six-segment horizontal strip fusing health + coverage counts into a single scan unit; attention + latest side-by-side below.

### Key choices
- Layout: single-line verdict → 6-cell strip → two-column below (attention / latest)
- The strip mixes verdict counts (passed/failed/blocked) with coverage counts (active/variants/run-7d) in one bar
- Flattest structure of the three — closest to the devices-page convention

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: maximum density, minimum scroll; everything visible above one fold on a laptop
- Weak at: fusing health + coverage into one strip blurs the two questions ("safe?" vs "maintained?"); verdict word is least prominent

### Best for
Operators who check the page many times a day and want the whole state in one glance.
